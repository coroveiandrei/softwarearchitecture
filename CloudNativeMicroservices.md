# Cloud-Native Microservices: From Domain to Production

> Stop building distributed monoliths. Learn to design, build, and operate cloud-native microservices that actually work — from first whiteboard sketch to production-ready system.

---

## Who is this for?

Development teams and architects moving from monolithic systems to microservices, or teams already working with microservices who want to do it right. No fluff, no slides-only theory — every concept is applied hands-on.

## What you'll walk away with

- A clear method to decompose your domain into services that make sense
- Contracts and communication patterns that won't break when you ship independently
- A fully automated pipeline: from code push to production with confidence
- The observability setup to actually know what's happening at 2 AM
- A running cloud-native microservice architecture you built yourself, deployed and orchestrated in the cloud

## Prerequisites

- Solid programming experience in at least one backend language (C#, Java, Python, or similar)
- Basic understanding of REST APIs and HTTP
- Familiarity with Git and version control workflows
- Basic experience with a cloud platform (e.g., Azure, AWS, or GCP — deploying an app or navigating the portal)
